# Privacy Policy — PokeNova Auction Bot

_Last updated: [fill in date]_

**Summary (for BotFather's short privacy policy field, if available):**
> POkeNova Auction Bot stores your Telegram user ID, name, and username, plus
> your auction activity (items listed, bids placed, verification status),
> to operate the auction system. Data is used only for bot functionality
> and is not sold or shared with third parties. Contact an admin via
> /report to request data deletion.

---

## 1. What data we collect

To operate the auction system, PokeNova Auction Bot collects and stores:

- Your Telegram **user ID**, **first/last name**, and **username**
- **Auction activity**: items you list, bids you place, auctions you win
  or lose, and your buyer/seller statistics
- **Verification status** (whether you've been verified via
  `/get_verified` or by an admin)
- **Moderation records**: whether you've been warned, banned, or reported,
  and by whom, for accountability purposes
- Messages you send to bot commands that require input (e.g. item
  details submitted via `/add`)

We do **not** collect or store the contents of your private messages
outside of what you explicitly submit through a bot command.

## 2. How we use your data

Your data is used only to:

- Run the auction system (tracking bids, items, and winners)
- Verify your identity within the auction group
- Maintain leaderboards (top sellers / top buyers)
- Enforce group rules (bans, warnings, moderation)
- Respond to support requests submitted via `/report`

## 3. Who can see your data

- **Admins and the bot owner** can view your profile, bid history, and
  moderation status through admin commands.
- **Other users** can see information you choose to make public through
  normal bot use — for example, your name next to a bid you placed, or
  your position on a public leaderboard.
- We do **not** sell, rent, or share your data with third parties or
  advertisers.

## 4. Data retention

Your data is retained for as long as you're an active participant in the
auction group, or as needed to maintain historical auction records (e.g.
past winners, bid history). You can request deletion of your data at any
time (see below).

## 5. Your rights

You can:

- Request a copy of the data we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data (note: this may remove your bid/auction
  history, which could affect ongoing auctions you're part of)

To make any of these requests, contact an admin using `/report`, or
message the bot owner directly.

## 6. Security

Data is stored using standard access controls, and only admins/owners
have access to moderation and management commands. As with any Telegram
bot, no data transmission method is 100% secure, but we take reasonable
steps to protect your information.

## 7. Children's privacy

This bot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect data from users under that age.

## 8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, an updated version will be posted here and
announced in the auction group.

## 9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent via `/report` in
the bot, or directly to the bot owner/admin team.
